FAQ

  • How do I play? Swipe left/right to hop to the rice in the next lane, swipe up to jump over chopsticks, and swipe down to duck under a sweeping hand.
  • How do I change the language? Tap the language button at the top left of the title screen.
  • How do I unlock other sushi? Beat your best score. Salmon, Tamago and Shrimp wake up at certain scores.
